IV · How It Works Page 05

A percentile-rank momentum oscillator.

The strategy doesn't predict price. It identifies when current momentum is in the top 35th percentile of recent momentum, then enters long. It exits when that ranking collapses. Six layers of risk filtering keep entries selective.

Most retail trading systems try to predict where price is going. This one doesn't. It measures the relative strength of current momentum compared to the recent 30-hour window, normalises that into a 0-to-1 score, and trades only when the score crosses above 0.65.

Stop predicting. Start ranking. The percentile-rank approach removes the need to guess price direction — it asks instead whether current momentum is unusually strong.

Five additional filters refine the entry: a moving-average spread captures sustained trend (not noise), a volatility band rejects both dead and spike markets, an RSI floor confirms momentum is broadly positive, an ATR-based stop limits tail losses, and a selective hour filter avoids two structurally noisy hours of the day (Asian liquidity trough and post-fix dealer chop).

The result: 32.9% win rate, but with average wins 3.4× larger than average losses. The math works because it's expectancy-positive, not because it's right often.

VI · Honest Risk Disclosure Page 07

What this system can't do.

This is not financial advice. Past performance — backtest or live — does not guarantee future results. Markets evolve and any quantitative system will eventually face conditions outside its training distribution.

Long-only exposure means bear-market vulnerability. The strategy doesn't short. During sustained gold downtrends, expect break-even or small losses (2021's +$89 illustrates the lower bound; a worse 18-month bear could produce a small annual loss).

Equity drawdown can briefly exceed 5%. WF07's largest floating loss was $572 (5.29% of $10K) before the trade closed in profit. You must trust the system through these moments. Manual intervention breaks the strategy.

Real broker conditions vary. Backtest used XM Ultra Low real-tick data with random delay. Other brokers, account types, or execution venues will produce different results. Forward demo testing on your actual broker is mandatory before live deployment.

The system requires patience. Win rate is 33%. You will see consecutive losing trades regularly. The math works only if the strategy is allowed to run uninterrupted across many trades.
